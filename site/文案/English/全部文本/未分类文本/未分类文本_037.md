## 3999867103

It was a long time ago. A boy disappeared, and then he returned to be a totally different person. That’s quite scary.
I used this excuse to distance myself from him.
At that time, I didn't know what would the consequence be, or how could I possibly deal with it.
But if I just stay as I was, nothing will change, I guess.
Same if I let it go.
The fear of facing something that is so different from what it was has made me let go of it.
I threw it away without a further look, without noticing what I have discarded.
That's why I ended up here.
That's why I felt overwhelmed when Kimi brought him "back".
The person who had been completely different is back to his old self as a different person.
My father calls this "Purification Ritual".
