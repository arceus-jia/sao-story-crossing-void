## 1571170731

Morpheus Realm
"The Dream Witch " is invading! Morpheus Realm now opens!
"The Dream Witch " Yidhra has the power of "immortality" and the ability to devour creatures to acquire their characteristics.
The only way to defeat her projection is through the collaboration of all Void Agents!



Requirement

Void Agent's level reaches Lv.60




Daily Character Challenge Limit

Due to Yidhra's devouring ability, each character has a daily challenge attempt limit in this mode.

1. Character challenge attempts will be consumed when an event stage is cleared.

2. Different qualities of the same character share daily challenge attempts.

3. The character with no challenge attempt left cannot challenge the event stages.

4. The character challenge attempts will be reset at 12:00 UTC daily.




Challenge Limit

There is a daily challenge attempt limit in this mode.

1. Formal Challenge only has 3 attempts per day;
2. Choose Practice Mode to simulate fights, practice mode will not deduct the number of times of character uses limit, and will not consume the challenges limit, but the results will not be saved.
3. The challenge limit will be reset at 12:00 UTC daily, and any unused times of the previous day will be expired.




Grouping

When Morpheus Realm is opened for the first time, all Void Agents will be randomly assigned to teams after entering this gameplay. And when it is opened subsequently, Void Agents will be assigned according to their latest performances of the previous season.

1. Void Agent from the same team will challenge all levels together before the end of the invasion.
2. During the invasion, the points earned by all Void Agents through formal challenges will be saved and ranked within the group.
3. At the end of the invasion, Void Agents' victory points will be determined based on their ranking, team rewards, and individual point rewards, and the Tier will be determined based on the victory points.




Tier

As the same as Decrepit Dream, there are 10 tiers:

1. After the invasion is over, the Morpheus Realm will be settled, and based on the  number of victory points obtained, the Tier may upgrade, remain unchanged, or demoted, etc.
2. After the settlement, Void Agent will receive the rewards corresponding to the current tier, the higher the tier, the more rewards will receive.





Claim Rewards
1. Individual points rewards: during the invasion, the points obtained from the formal challenge will be collected when the requirements are met.
2. Team Reward: During the invasion, all Void Agents in the group will receive the total points they have earned.
3. Clear Reward: when the level is cleared (all enemies' HP reaches 0), all Void Agent in the group will receive the Clear reward of the level, and the rewards will be automatically issued into the Inventory.
4. Tier rewards: sent by mail after the invasion is settled.
5. Unclaimed rewards: the victory point rewards will be expired if not claimed before the invasion settlement; the rest of the prop rewards will be sent by mail;

3. All the towers in the same event period share daily character challenge attempts.

Reward and Event Progress
