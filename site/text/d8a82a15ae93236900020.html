    2. Each maze contains 4 general Districts and 1 Boss District.

    3. Each time you complete a Normal Districts exploration, you will increase the number of maze exploration, which is shared within your faction.

   4. Each maze district exploration degree accumulates to a certain value will activate and enhance the Faction Buff, which can be viewed in the Town - Maze Information House.

    5. You can continue to explore the maze area even if it has reached 100% exploration degree.

    6. Each node exploration will cost 8 energy in the maze.

    7. Complete exploration of the maze district can get a final bonus treasure chest, which does not cost energy.

「Boss Challenges」

    1. Use Crystal Keys to challenge the World and Maze Boss.

    2. Crystal keys can be obtained by participating in the maze quest. You can hold up to 300 crystal keys.

    3. Maze Boss challenges require that all Normal Districts of the maze be fully explored first.

    4. Maze and World Boss HP is shared with your Faction.

    5. The leaderboard will record the highest score for a single official challenge.

    6. You can try to use practice mode for challenges, but no rewards will be given and no scores will be recorded.

「World Boss Special Instructions」

    1. Each world will have a World Boss to challenge.

    2. Challenging a World Boss requires that the Maze Bosses be fully cleared first.

    3. World Boss Challenge Progress is shared within your faction.

    4. World Bosses will be settled and move to the next world as long as either faction completes the challenge (World Boss HP = 0).

    5. World Boss Faction Challenge Rewards will be calculated based on the progress of the boss cleared, which means the HP remaining, by your faction at the time of settlement.

    6. After the last World Boss Challenge is completed, the final World Boss can be challenged to get Faction Challenge Scrolls, but there will no longer issue any settlement rewards.

「Attribute Restraint」

    1. Some of the enemy teams in this event have event attributes, and you may need to pay attention to the attribute restraint relationship.

    2. The specific attribute restraint relationships are: Water restrains Fire, Fire restrains Electricity, Electricity restrains Water, and Spirit and Physics restrain each other.

    3. Event Characters have their attributes, which can be viewed in the bistro.

    4. Event Equipment can add event attributes to your characters.

「Event Characters」

    1. Event characters can be recruited through Union Coins, and fight in the event challenge.

    2. Each event character has his or her event attributes, and you can decide the order of recruitment based on the actual situation.

    3. Event characters can only be used to participate in battles within this 2nd Anniversary Theme Event and will leave after the event is over.

「Breaking Events」

    1. Exploration in mazes may have a chance to trigger a breaking event.

    2. A special breaking event can be triggered by fighting with a special character a certain number of times in the Normal Districts of the maze.

    3. Special breaking events will reward Event Equipment.

「Event Equipment」

    1. Event Equipment can be obtained by special breaking events.

    2. Event Equipment can be enhanced and repaired by consuming materials to get stronger buffs.

    3. Event Equipment will automatically be bound to supported characters, and will take effect for both Event-recruited characters and characters you already have.

    4. Event Equipment will be recycled at the end of the event.

「Event Equipment Enhancement and Repair」

    1. Event Equipment can be enhanced by consuming energy cubes, which can be obtained by participating in maze exploration, mission, .etc.

    2. Different levels of energy cubes provide different enhancement success rates.

    3. A successful enhancement will increase the level and gain buff of the event equipment, while a failed reinforcement will remain no changes.

    4. The Remaining Enhancement Times of event equipment will be deducted whether the enhancement succeeds or fails.

    5. The Remaining Enhancement Times of the equipment can be filled by using Equipment Repair Oil, which can be obtained by participating in maze exploration, mission, .etc.

    6. Equipment will not be enhanced when it reaches the Max. level or when the Remaining Enhancement Times reaches 0.

    7. There is a limitation on the number of Remaining Enhancement Times and cannot be repaired when the limitation is reached.
