oldman： It's Giving Day today. This park is so remote that you can't even hear the sound of the festival. Only the elderly hang out here and ordinary tourists don't come out so far.

Miyuki Shiba： Honeymoon... with Onii-sama...

Tatsuya Shiba： We did receive a mission to eliminate the monsters, but there's more to it.

oldman： Oh? So what's your objective?

Tatsuya Shiba： We want to know what happened here five years ago. I heard that there was a great famine and many people died. Can you tell us more about it?

oldman： It was because of the monsters. The city was surrounded and there was no farming or fishing—inevitably, there was a famine.

Tatsuya Shiba： But in island cities like this, there are times when the storms roll in and fishing isn't possible. Surely there are food reserves.

Tatsuya Shiba： How could a famine start less than a month into the monster siege, causing so many of the elderly to die?

oldman： But that's what happened. Is this related to you outsiders?

Tatsuya Shiba： To us, this is important.

oldman： Cough Never mind. I'll be gone soon, so there's no point making it hard on you.

oldman： Since you've come so far, I'll tell you what I know. If you're not satisfied with the answer, you can ask the mayor. He knows more than us.

Tatsuya Shiba： Mayor?

oldman： Oh, he's called the archpriest now. A decent person, or so it seems.

Tatsuya Shiba： (Archpriest... The one who gave us the contradictory mission to eradicate the monsters.)

Tatsuya Shiba： Can we trust what the archpriest says?

oldman： Ah, it seems you have an understanding of the archpriest... Let me answer your previous question then. The cause of the famine was a fire.

Tatsuya Shiba： A fire?

oldman： Yes, a sudden fire burned the entire city's food reserves. The news spread among the people like wildfire and public order collapsed.

Tatsuya Shiba： What caused the fire?

oldman： No one knows. Maybe it was natural, maybe it was man-made. No one cared about the cause during the calamity—just trying to live until the next day was exhausting enough.

oldman： People became increasingly paranoid and hostile. Stealing and only looking out for themselves. Everyone had to be merciless to keep themselves alive.

oldman： The famine lasted for a month, and the elderly like me didn't eat so our families could have more food...

oldman： Those without families had their little amounts of food stolen... This kind of stuff continued until "that" happened.

Tatsuya Shiba： You mean the miracle?

oldman： No, I mean Mia's thing. The miracle came after. Us old homeless folk are still alive because of Mia.

oldman： But why, why did this happen...?

Miyuki Shiba： Who's Mia?

oldman： She was the stonemason Murray's daughter and the archpriest's niece. Murray's wife is the archpriest's sister. She died giving birth to Mia...

oldman： Mia was just like her mother. A lively, cheerful, and kind girl.

oldman： During the famine, Mia often came to the park to deliver food to the old people who almost starved to death.

Miyuki Shiba： Oh...

oldman： I often think some people in this world should die to balance out the scale of suffering they have caused.

oldman： But Mia never did a single bad thing. She was such a kind and good child...

Miyuki Shiba： What happened to the child?
