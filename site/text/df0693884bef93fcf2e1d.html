note： Apr 24

note： I picked up a puppy on the beach today and named him Rohse, which means "sunny" on Clante. I named him Rohse because he was surrounded by a glow when I found him.

note： I was so shocked I dropped my diary into the water when I saved Rohse. I'll call this new diary "Rohse's Diary".

note： Apr 26

note： Rohse looks very weak and has been lying on his stomach. I thought he was injured, but I couldn't find the wound. Is he sick? Hope he'll get better.

note： Apr 27

note： I secretly asked Mr. Ray for help. He's a famous doctor on the island. But even he didn't know what happened to Rohse.

note： Apr 29

note： Daddy found Rohse. Mr. Ray told Uncle Allen, who then told Daddy. Daddy said Rohse was sick and had to be thrown away. No way! Sigh, I wish Mom was here.

note： May 3

note： Rohse got better and started to run around the house. Daddy finally agreed to let me keep Rohse. I knew if I cried long enough, Daddy would listen to me.

note： May 5

note： Amazing! Amazing! A big discovery! Rohse turned my leftover bread into stone! Unbelievable! I knew Rohse wasn't just any other dog! I'm gonna tell Daddy! ... But he's coming home late today.

note： May 8

note： Daddy didn't come home. Uncle Allen had someone send food to us. The bread was fluffy and the soup was yummy. Rohse loved them. But I like Daddy's food better. I don't know why. I just do.

note： May 12

note： Seems like Daddy's been busy. But I don't feel lonely because Rohse is with me. He sometimes turns my important things into stone, though...

note： Whenever I get angry with Rohse, he'll look at me pitifully with its tail hanging down, and I'll always forgive him... Oh, and he looks a lot like Daddy.

note： May 15

note： Daddy is back. Looks like something happened in the forest and a lot of people have gone missing. Rohse's been a good boy, so Daddy said he'd buy him a nice collar. I'm so happy.

note： But Daddy doesn't believe Rohse is a magic dog. Rohse was being bad. I told him to turn things into stone in front of Daddy, but he wouldn't.

note： May 17

note： Rohse's amazing. He seems to understand me. I took him out today. He was being a very good boy.

note： May 20

note： The grown-ups look very busy these days. They keep saying things like "more people died today" and "food shortage". I don't get it. Isn't there a lot of food in the house? But Daddy told me not to tell anyone about it.

note： May 25

note： Daddy brought plenty of food back again. So much Daddy and I can't eat it all... The basement is already packed full, but Daddy still looked sad. Said there was too much food and too little space.

note： Daddy told me to stay home. I'm happy as long as I have food. I wonder what's going on outside.

note： May 27

note： I heard there were monsters in the city. Daddy told me to never go out. Argh, so bored. I wanna go out, even for just one day, to see what the monsters look like. Rohse just jumped on the table again.

note： Whenever I write my diary these days, Rohse will jump onto the table. Does it read my diary? But can he understand? Hehe, I love my smart boy Rohse. Does he understand this sentence too?

note： May 28

note： Daddy finally came home after leaving for three days. He looked tired. I know Mr. Ray and Daddy have been helping Uncle Allen. I'm a good girl, so I didn't bother Daddy.

note： Rohse was being good and stayed quiet. It's just that he's always making stone. But the strange pattern on the stone is lovely, so I started carrying it with me.

note： Jun 1

note： There was a fire in the city. The biggest granary was burned down. The grown-ups looked pale. Some cried, "we're done for." Was it because of the monsters? I don't want to know or watch. If only the bad monsters would just go away.

note： Jun 2

note： Rohse is gone. I wanted to go out and look for him, but I dared not. He didn't come back until the evening. Amazing how he jumped all the way to the second floor and through the window. No matter where he goes, he always comes back to me.

note： Jun 5

note： I'm so sad. Daddy's always not home, and Rohse goes out during the day too. I'm all by myself. If only the bad monsters would go away, so we could go back to normal. I want to go out and play with Rohse!

note： Jun 6

note： Rohse left home again today. I couldn't help going out to look for him. I found lots of old people in the park. They were lying on the ground and looked so hungry.

note： Jun 7

note： I tried to bake bread today but burned quite a lot of it. I kept the black bits for dinner and gave the good bread to those old people in the park.

note： They looked so happy. An old man even cried. But... bad Rohse turned my bread into stone! I know it was burned and tasted awful, but he couldn't just turn it into stone! Rohse, bad!

note： Jun 9

note： I went to the park again today, and I made much better bread. I was so happy talking to the old people. I told them Rohse can turn bread into stone, but they didn't believe me and said only a mage could do that. I know Rohse is special.

note： Jun 11

note： Awful! As always, I went to the park and gave bread to the old people. On my way back, I ran into a monster. A huge one with a big mouth and shiny eyes.

note： There was no one around. I was so scared I crouched down and couldn't move. Then I remembered I had one of Rohse's stones in my pocket. I threw it hard at the monster! Guess what?

note： The monster suddenly stopped moving and left with the stone in its mouth!

note： Jun 12

note： Daddy came home today with Uncle Allen. He said I made good bread. It was just leftover bread after I came back from the park, though.

note： I asked Uncle Allen what happened on the island and whether we could go back to normal. He said we would, but he didn't believe Rohse is a magic dog either.

note： Jun 15

note： I've been very busy. So much more old people show up in the park. But I'm happy as long as I can help them out. There are younger people, though. They grabbed my food. I don't like them.

note： Jun 16

note： Someone was breaking things downstairs today. I was so scared and kept hiding on the second floor. When I went down, all the windows were open and the doors were broken. The house was in a mess. They rummaged through the house for food. There was food scattered around.

note： Good thing the food in the basement is safe. Only Daddy and I know the entrance. Did they do that because of the monsters or...?

note： Jun 18

note： I bumped into a stranger on my way to the park. I said sorry, but he still took away my bread. I was sad and angry. I told the old people and they said I shouldn't come to the park again. But why?

note： If I don't go, the old people will get hungry again... Daddy and Uncle Allen brought so much food home. It'd be unfair if I kept all of it. Right, Rohse...?

note： Jun 20
