[Event Time]

2022/4/1 12:00 - 2022/5/1 11:59 UTC

[Continental Adventure]

There are three regions in Trowa:

[Borderland], [Álfheimr], and [Ατλαντίς],

each with its own towns, in which the Void agents will be randomly born in one of the regions.

Get the unique regional vehicle and drive it to set off your adventurous journey in Trowa.

*If the void agent has the invitation letter of one of the regions, the agent will be born in the corresponding region.

[Town Fame]

Clear the [Resource Quest] stages,

you can acquire the Fame of the town

and gain various buff bonus!

Increases your Fame at [Hodur], [Newton], [Darwin] to unlock new vehicles.

[Town Trading]

Each town owns a [Trade Center] where void agents can trade goods to accumulate funds.

The same goods will have different prices in the [Trade Center] of different towns.

Look for the best town to bid on!

Each town's [Trade Center] has its own specialty items that can be unlocked by increasing the town's Fame!

Additional funds points are earned when selling goods in towns.

Accumulate funds points to claim great rewards!

[Town Investment]

Void agents can invest money in each town and become shareholders of the town!

After becoming a shareholder, you can enjoy the shareholder funds dividends after the daily settlement ~

[Resource Collection]

Each town has a [Resource Quest] stage.

Raid the stages, collect goods & town reputation!

[Defeat The World Enemys]

All void agents can raid the World Enemys together.

Void agents weaken the spirit of the World Enemy by dealing with DMG in the battle.

Challenge the [World Enemy] to gain contribution points.

Accumulate contribution points to reap great rewards!

[Repair the Babel]

Challenge [World Enemy] to collect the restoration materials.

Put the materials into the corresponding parts

to increase the repairing progress & gain contribution points.

[Repair Parts]

There are three parts of the [Babel] that need to be repaired,

and each part requires different materials.

[Restoration Rewards]

The parts will activate gain buffs when the repair work is complete,

and bonus rewards will be given for the complete repair of the [babel]!

[Achievements]

Closed: 2022/5/4 11:59 UTC

After accumulating [Town Fame], [Funds Points],

and [Contribution Points] to the certain amount,

you can receive great rewards respectively, so don't miss out ~
