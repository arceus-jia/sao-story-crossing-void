Miyuki Shiba： Onii-sama, do we not have to clear the monsters?

Tatsuya Shiba： No, we have a different objective. Our mission is to investigate the source of the stones.

Miyuki Shiba： Investigating in the city?

Tatsuya Shiba： Yeah. Oread suspects there may be someone behind the scenes and I agree.

Tatsuya Shiba： If we want to find out who it is, we have to look for people in the know. This city was the epicenter of the calamity after all.

Tatsuya Shiba： Speaking of which, when I walk through these streets, something feels off to me.

Miyuki Shiba： Off...? It only seems like an ordinary and popular tourist island...

Tatsuya Shiba： Yes, ordinary—perhaps too ordinary. I'd never have thought it was ravaged by monsters just five years ago.

Tatsuya Shiba： Even though the tourism industry is booming now, it used to be a poor island. The only industry seems to have been fishing.

Tatsuya Shiba： And they developed it so much in only five years?

Tatsuya Shiba： Besides, where did all the old people in the city go? Even if they stay home most of the time, they must come out during the festival.

Miyuki Shiba： You're right. We haven't seen any old people since we set off.

Tatsuya Shiba： Yes. Even though I heard many of the elderly died of hunger in the calamity five years ago, it seems unlikely there would be no elderly at all.

Tatsuya Shiba： And now, the whole island is thriving—enough to hold such a grand festival.

Tatsuya Shiba： It doesn't feel quite right.

Miyuki Shiba： I heard the archpriest brought in a lot of food aid from outside to lift the famine...

Miyuki Shiba： Without foreign aid, it's hard to imagine such development in a few short years.

Tatsuya Shiba： Right. I had a quick search and found no record of foreign countries or organizations assisting the island.

Tatsuya Shiba： This is wildly different from what the locals told us.

Tatsuya Shiba： My instinct tells me the elderly who survived the famine may know the reason.

Miyuki Shiba： Maybe they got rid of the elderly in the city to cover up the real reason behind the island's revival...?

Tatsuya Shiba： Perhaps. Miyuki, let's go.

Miyuki Shiba： Onii-sama, where are we going?

Tatsuya Shiba： A little further from the city... Hmmm, let's take a look in the park.

Miyuki Shiba： Park? Are we not going to the festival?

Tatsuya Shiba： People who have lost their footing will always gather in the park. Of course, I checked that there are no elderly at the festival grounds.

Tatsuya Shiba： A park in the outskirts far from the festival may well be their gathering place.
