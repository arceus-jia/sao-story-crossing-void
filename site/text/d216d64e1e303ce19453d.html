[Facility Training]

Facility training is divided into [General Training]&[Special Training].

Each support character can participate in 5 types of facilities training,

each training will bring different attribute growth to the character.

[General Training]

Consume [Energy] to participate in General Training,

obtain attributes to grow up.

There are 5 types of facility training: [Swimming], [Water Fight],

[Sunbathing], [Surfing] and [Crafting].

Each facility has its facility level, so you can accumulate training

times to improve training level and improve training efficiency!

In addition, each facility has three levels of difficulty.

The higher the difficulty, the higher the training efficiency.

Each character has two preferred attributes,

which gain extra growth during General Training.

[Special Training]

Consume [Training Tickets] to participate in Special Training,

gain lots of attributes to grow up.

There are 5 types of facility training: [Swimming], [Water Fight],

[Sunbathing], [Surfing] and [Crafting].

In addition, each facility has three levels of difficulty.

The higher the difficulty, the higher the training efficiency.

[Types of Facility]

[Swimming]: Endurance + a few Will

[Water Fight]: Intuition + a few Endurance

[Sunbathing]: Will + a few Taste

[Surfing]: Intelligence + a few Intuition

[Crafting]: Taste + a few Intelligence

「Fatigue Value」

Participating in General Training will accumulate Fatigue Value

and affect the efficiency of General Training.

Fatigue Value 0-12000: not affected

Fatigue Value 12001-18000: efficiency reduced by 30%

Fatigue Value 18001-21000: efficiency reduced by 60%

Fatigue Value 21001-24000: efficiency reduced by 80%

Fatigue Value >24000: efficiency reduced by 95%

*Every day at 12:00 UTC, the character will take a break,

and the Fatigue Value will become 0

*Fatigue Value will not affect Special Training

[Training Pt]

Participating in [General Training]&[Special Training] will

accumulate Training Pts, which are the basis for [Support Ranking].

「Training Events」

Clear event dungeons with specific characters, chance to

trigger [Training Events], help the character gain various growth!
