Shiina Mashiro： Kuroneko?

Kuroneko： What should I do...

Shiina Mashiro： Kuroneko? Are you alive?

Kuroneko： Nonsense, of course, I'm alive. I'm just thinking.

Kuroneko： Ah, Mashiro. Are you here to buy books too?

Shiina Mashiro： Mhmm. I need to do some research to make a manga.

Kuroneko： Research... You've bought quite a few. Are they all manga?

Shiina Mashiro： Manga has everything.

Kuroneko： Hehe, that's true. Hmm? What's this?

Shiina Mashiro： Hmm?

Kuroneko： W-What? Isn't this Maschera's New Year Special Edition! It's a forbidden book! There's an alternate storyline that is a sealed publication!

Kuroneko： How did you get it?

Shiina Mashiro： I just got it from the store.

Kuroneko： What?!

Shiina Mashiro： I heard it was the last one so I bought it.

Kuroneko： I lost by a hair...

Shiina Mashiro： Do you want to read it, Kuroneko?

Kuroneko： I do! But how could I? I'm a master of Maschera. Why should I...

Shiina Mashiro： Didn't you want to read the sealed alternate storyline or something?

Kuroneko： G-Geez... Are you the devil? Stop tempting me.

Shiina Mashiro： Here.

Kuroneko： Why?

Shiina Mashiro： This manga is a gift to you. But after you read it, lend it to me so I can read the contents.

Kuroneko： Mm... If you're giving it to me, then I'm going to unseal the pages then. Is that okay?

Shiina Mashiro： The book will be glad that its first reader is Kuroneko.

Kuroneko： The book will be... glad...

Shiina Mashiro： Yep. The book must hope that the first person to open it is also the person most looking forward to reading it.

Shiina Mashiro： —Or are you actually not that interested in reading it?

Kuroneko： I am! Of course, I am.

Kuroneko： Alright then, I will accept the book but in exchange, I will buy a manga for you of my choosing. That way, we're all square, okay?

Shiina Mashiro： Okay.

Kuroneko： Hehehe... I will bathe and change into special clothes when I get home... Then I will have to have an unsealing ceremony—done properly, of course.
